Privacy policy

EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 27, 2026

NovaEdge Tech Corp ("NovaEdge Tech", "we") is a software and network engineering company based in Florida, USA. This policy covers our website, novaedgetechnology.com, and the app we use to publish to our own Facebook Page.

The short version

  • We only collect what you send us.
  • We set no cookies and run no analytics or ad tracking. The contact form has a bot check from Cloudflare.
  • We never sell personal information.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

What we collect

Contact form. When you send the form we receive your name, your email address and your message, along with the time and an approximate location (city, region and country, estimated from your IP address). It reaches us as an email, and we use it to reply to you and to work with you.

Bot check on the form. To keep bots out, the contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile. When you start filling in the form, Turnstile runs a check in your browser to confirm that a person is there. For that, Cloudflare receives your IP address and details about your browser and device, and may store data in your browser (cookies or local storage). We only learn whether the check passed. Cloudflare explains how it handles this information in its Turnstile Privacy Addendum.

Email and phone. If you email or call us, we keep what you share so we can respond.

Visiting the site. We set no cookies and run no analytics or advertising trackers. The bot check above only runs if you start filling in the contact form. Like any website, our hosting provider processes your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages and keep them secure. The site also asks our host for your approximate location (city level, from your IP address) so the 3D globe can turn toward you and the sky can match your local time. That is used in your browser during the visit and we do not store it.

Our Facebook app

We use an app on Meta's platform to automate posts to our own Facebook Page. It publishes our content to that Page and does nothing else. It does not collect or store personal information about people on Facebook, including people who see, like or comment on our posts. What you do on Facebook is covered by Meta's own privacy policy.

Who helps us

A few companies process information for us, only to provide their service:

  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Resend delivers contact form messages to our inbox.
  • Cloudflare runs our domain, routes our email and provides the contact form's bot check (Turnstile).
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.
  • Meta provides our Facebook Page and the platform our app runs on.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Messages stay in our inbox for as long as we need them to answer you, do the work and keep business records. After that we delete them, or sooner if you ask.

Data deletion

You can ask us to delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. Email jose.sierra@novaedgetechnology.com or call (305) 707-8670 and tell us what you want removed. We will delete it and confirm, normally within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep something.

You can also ask what we hold about you, or have it corrected, the same way.

Children

Our site and services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.
